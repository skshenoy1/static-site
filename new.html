Welcome

Welcome to our student housing search platform designed to make finding affordable, convenient housing in the Research Triangle easier than ever.

Problem

Finding affordable housing that matches a student's budget, location, and lifestyle preferences is challenging. Most housing sites are too generic and time-consuming. Our site solves this by collecting simple user input and providing tailored housing recommendations.
